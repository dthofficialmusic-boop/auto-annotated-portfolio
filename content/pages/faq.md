    text: >+
      Q: What does Cotidor provide for artists?


      A: Cotidor provides a plethora of services to managed artists. To find an
      in-depth description of our services, head on to our [information
      page.](/info)




      Q: Does Cotidor charge an artist for its services?


      A: Cotidor does not — and never will charge artists for money, ask for
      financial compensation, or take a cut from royalties made from singles.
      Our services are free of charge, as long as the said artist complies with
      our terms.




      Q: Does Cotidor accept any artists?


      A: Yes, Cotidor accepts any artists — all genres, art forms, genders, and
      even AI artists are welcome to apply.




      Q: I want to apply as an artist. How do I go about doing so?


      A: A form is seen at the homepage of our website, to which you can submit
      details surrounding your information. Alternatively, you may use our
      [satellite form](https://form.typeform.com/to/WTYU35lF) — through this
      medium, applicants typically receive their results a day earlier than
      those who send their applications on our website.




      Q: How long will the processing of my application take?


      A: The wait times for applications are typically 1 - 3 days. This gives
      our recruitment department ample time to review an applicant's details.




      Q: It has been more than three days since my application. How do I follow
      up?


      A: Though unlikely, if your application has been pending for more than 3
      days, you can contact: info\@cotidor.com or admin\@cotidor.com, and we'll
      have our reviewing process expedited.
